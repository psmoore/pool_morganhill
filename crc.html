CRC Aquatics

Heated indoor pool · three 20-yard lap lanes · play area and slide

Lap swim, swim lessons, aqua fitness and open play in the Centennial Recreation Center pool. 171 W Edmundson Avenue · (408) 782-2128.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Each block shows how many lap lanes are open.

Three lanes, counted for you

The CRC schedule carries its most useful fact in small print: “(3 Lap Lanes Open)”, “(1 Lap Lane Open)”, “(No Lap Lanes Open)”, and on Wednesday “(3 Lap Lane Open until 4:15pm) (1 Lap Lane Open from 4:15pm–6:45pm)”.

On the calendar each of those is its own block with its own lane count, so a lap swimmer sees at a glance that Wednesday at 5 means one lane beside the lessons, and that the pool is closed from 11 until 2.

Play area and slide The shallow play area (1 to 2.5 ft) is open whenever the pool is; its water features are off during swim lessons and aqua fitness. The large slide opens Saturdays, 12:00–4:30pm.
