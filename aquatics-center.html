Morgan Hill Aquatics Center

Open for lap swim and aqua fitness · summer season from June 5, 2027

An Olympic-size competition pool and a warm-water instructional pool, open year round; the recreation pool, slides and spray-ground open for summer. 16200 Condit Road · (408) 782-2134.

The modification is already on the calendar

The Aquatics Center page says “Closures and Modified Programs: Stay tuned for future updates.” The one modification that exists is a sentence at the bottom of page two of the schedule PDF: lap swim is 5–7am on Saturday, October 24, because of a swim meet.

Page to October 24 and it's there, on the day, in the same place as every other Saturday. The next change goes in the same way, and the PDF doesn't need re-exporting.

Questions for the aquatics office

Everything here was transcribed from what Morgan Hill publishes. These are the places where the sources disagree, go quiet, or where we had to make a call — each one is also written on the calendar entry itself.

  • ConflictDeep H2O Dynamics at 11am — which lanes?
    The class runs weekdays 11:00–11:55 in the Competition Pool, inside the 5:00am–1:30pm lap swim block. The schedule gives lap swim “9–17 lanes” but never says which lanes the class takes. The calendar flags it until you say how the pool splits.
  • GapThe October 24 swim meet
    Lap swim that Saturday is 5–7am, and the calendar already shows it. The make-up Sunday in October has no date yet, and the notice doesn't say whether Saturday water polo and the Instructional Pool run that day.
  • OursSplash Youth Swim — which lanes, and each group's times?
    The team page gives Monday–Thursday “between 4:15 and 7:55pm” in the Competition Pool, which is exactly when public lap swim drops to 5 lanes. We put Splash on the other 12 (lanes 6–17) as one block. Each group's times, and the time of Intro to Water Polo on Mondays and Wednesdays, would show the real practices.
  • Conflict17 lanes or 18?
    The aquatics page says the Competition Pool offers “a minimum of 5 lanes to 18 lanes at a time”. The schedule tops out at 17. We built the pool with 17.
  • Ours“9–17 lanes” shown as 9
    Weekday morning lap swim is shown on the 9 lanes the schedule guarantees, and Saturday's “10–17” on 10. When do the other lanes open to lap swim? Say, and the calendar shows each extra lane at the hour it opens.
  • OursAqua Zumba on the first Saturday
    The schedule notes Aqua Zumba “on the 1st Saturday of the month” in the Saturday 8am Shallow Tone slot. We read that as Aqua Zumba replacing Shallow Tone that day, and noted it on the class.
