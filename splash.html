Morgan Hill Splash Youth Swim

Ages 5–18 · swimming and water polo · Competition Pool

A year-round recreational program built on fun, family and fundamentals. Four practices a week, Monday–Thursday between 4:15 and 7:55pm, plus Intro to Water Polo on Mondays and Wednesdays. $100 a month with an active CRC membership.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Splash practices Monday–Thursday, 4:15–7:55pm, on 12 lanes of the Competition Pool.

The other twelve lanes

From 4 to 8pm, Monday to Thursday, the pool schedule gives public lap swim 5 lanes of 17. On Friday, when Splash doesn't practice, lap swim gets 11. The team is the difference, and the pool schedule never mentions it.

On the calendar the evening pool adds up: Splash on 12 lanes, lap swim on 5, all 17 accounted for. With each group's own times, parents would see their swimmer's slot and lap swimmers would see exactly which lanes are theirs.
